Straight answers, before you ask

What must my employer prove?

Solvency (6 months of statements averaging ≥ 100 minimum wages), an official-format contract and a letter justifying hiring you over a Colombian. We coordinate directly with your company.

What if I change jobs?

The visa dies with the contract: a new employer or role requires a new visa, and working elsewhere revokes it. That's why the initial contract must be negotiated right from day one.

Does my foreign degree count?

Yes, apostilled and translated; or experience certifications backing you. For regulated professions in Colombia (health, engineering, law…), you also need the council license.

Does it lead to residency?

Yes. It's an M visa: 5 continuous years as a holder let you apply for the Resident visa. Ideal if your plan outlasts the contract.

How long and from where do I apply?

Usually 2 to 5 weeks once filed. From Colombia or your country of residence or nationality.

Do I need a lawyer if it's all online?

No platform requires counsel. But this visa gets reviewed with a magnifying glass on both sides — company and worker — and a poorly answered requirement delays your start date. For your work move, it's worth shielding.
