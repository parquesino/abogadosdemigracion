Spouse Visa requirements

The file Cancillería expects to see — we build and verify it with you, document by document.

  • Colombian civil marriage certificate (both sides), faithful copy of the original, issued under 3 months ago.
  • Colombian spouse's citizenship ID.
  • Real-relationship proof: 4–8 dated photos on different occasions + social/chats screenshots.
  • Shared life: tickets, hotel bookings, transfers, joint accounts or contracts.
  • Motivation letter signed by your Colombian spouse, grounded in your story.
  • Authenticated special POA signed by your spouse, authorizing you to apply.
  • Migratory movements for both, issued by Migración Colombia.

Married outside Colombia? The certificate must come apostilled, officially translated and registered here before filing — we handle that too.

Straight answers, before you ask

Married outside Colombia — does it count?

Yes, with one prior step: the foreign certificate must come apostilled, officially translated into Spanish and registered at a Colombian consulate or notary before filing. We handle that for you.

Not married? Common-law partner?

A notary- or court-declared unión marital de hecho grants exactly the same M-visa rights. On the consultation we assess which route — marriage or union — is fastest and safest for you.

Do same-sex couples qualify?

Fully yes. Colombia recognizes equal marriage and same-sex de facto unions, with identical rights and the same process.

What are the motivation letter and special POA?

Cancillería requires a formal letter signed by the Colombian spouse requesting the visa, plus an authenticated special POA authorizing the foreigner to apply. We draft both around your real story.

How long and from where do I apply?

Usually 2 to 5 weeks once filed. From Colombia or your country of residence or nationality, 100% online.

Do I need a lawyer if it's all online?

No platform requires counsel. But decisions are discretionary: they can ask for extra proof or inadmit a complete file. To reunite with the one you love, the letter, the proof and the requirement responses make the difference.
