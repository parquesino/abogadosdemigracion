Rentista Visa requirements

The file Cancillería expects to see — we build and verify it with you, document by document.

  • Passive-income certificate of ≥ 10 monthly minimum wages (recognized public entity, company or financial institution, or property rents), issued under 3 months ago, apostilled.
  • 6 months of bank statements showing regular deposits of that income.
  • Criminal records from where you lived the last 3 years, apostilled with official translation.
  • Health insurance covering your stay: emergencies, accidents, hospitalization and repatriation.

Straight answers, before you ask

What counts as passive income?

Rents, dividends, interest, royalties: anything that doesn't require your daily work. It must total ≥ 10 monthly minimum wages, certified by a recognized entity and visible in 6 months of statements.

Can I work on this visa?

No: it bars working for or billing Colombian companies and receiving local pay. Your Colombian investment income is allowed.

Does it lead to residency?

It doesn't accrue toward residency. If your goal is staying years, the consultation assesses whether Retirement, Spouse or another M suits you better — before you invest in the wrong visa.

How long and from where do I apply?

Two to five weeks once filed. From Colombia or your country of residence or nationality. Granted duration (6 months to 2 years) is discretionary.

Can I include my family?

Yes: spouse and children under 25 as dependents, each with their fees and a reduced legal fee.

Do I need a lawyer if it's all online?

No platform requires counsel. But proving irregular or multi-country income before a discretionary officer is exactly where files get inadmitted. For your Colombian adventure, it's worth shielding.

What if my application is inadmitted?

We re-file it with no legal fees, as many times as needed until approval. Each new $56 USD official study is paid to the State. Final rejections not covered.
