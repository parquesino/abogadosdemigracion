Straight answers, before you ask

What if Cancillería asks for more documents?

A requirement is not a denial. Your lawyer answers promptly with legal tactics. The Protected plan covers up to two requirements; the Turnkey plan covers them all.

What if my application is inadmitted?

Fixable errors get re-filed: Essential doesn't include re-application ($80 per re-filing), Protected includes 1 with no legal fees, and Turnkey includes all needed ones with even the official study fee covered. Only Essential and Protected pay each new $56 official study. A final rejection closes the case, with a 6-month wait for a new process.

Are government fees included?

In Essential and Protected they're paid separately to the State (study $56, issuance V $183 / M $279 / R $408, cédula $294,000 COP). In Turnkey they're included.

How long does the process really take?

Doing it alone, expect 14 to 17 weeks: learning curve, apostilles, the SITAC platform and high risk of inadmissions (pay and restart) or rejections (6-month ban). With our firm, we recommend starting 7 weeks before your trip or permit expiry — we audit every document, manage the platforms and speed up the consular stage.

Can I apply while in Colombia on a tourist permit?

Yes, with a current permit. What's critical is that your foreign documents arrive apostilled: criminal records and certifications (pension, marriage, income per the visa). Without apostille, the application gets rejected.

How current must my documents be?

Criminal records must be under 90 days from issuance. US citizens note: Cancillería requires the federal FBI Identity History Summary — state or local certificates are NOT accepted. Every non-Spanish document needs official Spanish translation.
