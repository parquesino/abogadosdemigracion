The Business Owner Visa in numbers

M-type visa for investors in a Colombian business — new or existing. Apply from Colombia or your country.

Minimum investment100 min. wages ($174.7M COP ≈ $53,750 USD)
Duration1 to 3 years
Work allowed?Yes — in your own company
DependentsSpouse & children under 25
Path to residency5 accrued years → R visa
Official fees$56 study + $279 issuance + $294,000 cédula

Owner vs. Investor: if your money goes into company shares or equity, this is your visa (from 100 minimum wages, and it lets you work in it). The Investor visa is for pure financial capital or real estate (from 350/650 minimum wages). The consultation confirms which one applies.

Business Owner Visa requirements

The file Cancillería expects to see — we build it with you and your accountant, document by document.

  • Share certificate signed by a public accountant: capital registered and paid in your name for ≥ 100 minimum wages, issued under 3 months ago.
  • Investment traceability: deposits from your account to the company, invoices, contributions and equipment.
  • Accountant's documents: citizenship ID, professional card and current professional-background certificate.
  • Company financial health: 4 months of statements, current existence & representation certificate, employees with social security current, payroll, taxes, leases and vendors.

Stake changes or company non-compliance affect the visa — we structure it so that never catches you by surprise.

Straight answers, before you ask

How much must I invest in my company?

At least 100 minimum wages registered and paid in your name ($174,688,200 COP ≈ $53,750 USD in 2026), certified by a public accountant with full funds traceability.

Can I work in my company?

Yes: this visa expressly authorizes working in the company you own or co-own. But it's tied to that stake: selling it or company non-compliance affects the visa.

Must my company be fully compliant?

Cancillería reviews current existence, statements, employees with social security current, payroll, taxes, leases and vendors. We pre-audit everything with your accountant before filing.

Does it lead to residency?

Yes. It's an M visa: 5 continuous years as a holder let you apply for the Resident visa.

How long and from where do I apply?

Usually 2 to 5 weeks once filed. From Colombia or your country of residence or nationality.

Do I need a lawyer if it's all online?

No platform requires counsel. But this is the most document-heavy visa of all — accountant, traceability, company health — and an accounting gap inadmites the case. For your business, it's worth shielding.
