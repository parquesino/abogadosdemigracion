Straight answers, before you ask

Do I complete the time with my history?

5 continuous years on any M visa, or 2 years on a Spouse or Colombian-parent visa — always with current documents, no gaps. The consultation audits your exact timeline.

What if I traveled a lot?

Cancillería reviews migratory movements closely: long absences can break continuity. Bring your history to the consultation and we'll tell you if you qualify or how much is left.

Can I work and bring my family?

Yes to both: permanent residents work freely and cover dependents (art. 3 Res. 5477), each with their fees.

And after the R?

The R lasts 5 years (then transferred) and 5 years as a resident open Colombian citizenship. The final stretch.

How long and from where do I apply?

Usually 2 to 5 weeks once filed. From Colombia or your country of residence or nationality.

Do I need a lawyer if it's all online?

No platform requires counsel. But this visa is won or lost on the letter and history continuity — exactly where discretion bites hardest. For your definitive residency, it's worth shielding.
