Parents-of-Colombians requirements

The file Cancillería expects to see — we build and verify it with you, document by document.

  • Child's Colombian birth record with the nationality annotation and database presence (plus prior records for any other annotations).
  • Request letter for your case: signed by the Colombian parent (minor), by both parents (both foreigners), or by the adult child with fingerprint + their ID.
  • Domicile-granting visa proof at the child's birth: Cancillería verifies and abstains on inconsistencies.
  • Migratory movements for the foreign parent (and the Colombian one if applicable), from Migración Colombia.
  • Economic solvency: 6 months of statements with average balances and income sources.

Straight answers, before you ask

What if both parents are foreigners?

You still qualify, as long as you held a domicile-granting visa at birth: both parents sign the letter and the record must carry the nationality annotation.

What if the other parent is absent?

A family-authority certification is needed: no protection or rights-restitution measures, and proof you've met your obligations. We handle it.

Does my adult child count the same?

Yes: they sign the letter themselves (signature + fingerprint) requesting your M visa and attach their Colombian ID.

Can I work and bring family?

Yes to both: it authorizes any legal activity and covers dependents (children under 25 and dependents).

How long and from where do I apply?

Usually 2 to 5 weeks once filed. From Colombia or your country of residence or nationality.

Do I need a lawyer if it's all online?

No platform requires counsel. But the civil record and domicile proof at birth are exactly where Cancillería inadmites without mercy. To raise your child here, it's worth shielding.
