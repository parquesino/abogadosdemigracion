Duration1 to 3 years
Work allowed?Not expressly banned — assessed
Path to residency5 accrued years → R visa
Official fees$56 study + $279 issuance + $294,000 cédula

Watch out for confusion #1: if your money goes into a company or business (shares, equity), the right visa is the Business Owner visa (from 100 minimum wages), not this one. And the investment must be maintained and reported: changes affect the visa.

Investor Visa requirements

The file Cancillería expects to see — we build and verify it with you, document by document.

  • Banco de la República registration of direct investment in your name over 650 minimum wages, or certificate of tradition and liberty for solely-owned real estate of at least 350 minimum wages + BanRep purchase registration.
  • 3 months of bank statements proving solvency to remain in the country.
  • Investment maintenance proof if you held this visa before.
  • Health coverage in Colombia: accidents, illness, hospitalization and repatriation during your stay.

Straight answers, before you ask

Exactly how much must I invest?

Over 650 minimum wages in registered direct investment ($1,135,473,300 COP ≈ $349,380 USD at Oct 2026) or at least 350 minimum wages in solely-owned real estate ($611,408,700 COP ≈ $188,130 USD), always with Banco de la República registration.

Does my company investment count?

Not for this visa: equity investments go through the Business Owner visa (from 100 minimum wages, and it lets you work in it). The consultation confirms which one is yours before you move a peso.

Can I work on this visa?

The rule doesn't expressly ban it — in practice it usually allows any legal activity — but officer discretion means we can't guarantee it in writing on the visa. We assess your case.

Does it lead to residency?

Yes. It's an M visa: 5 continuous years as a holder let you apply for the Resident visa, as long as you maintain and report the investment.

How long and from where do I apply?

Usually 2 to 5 weeks once filed. From Colombia or your country of residence or nationality.

Do I need a lawyer if it's all online?

No platform requires counsel. But FX registration, tradition & liberty and funds traceability are exactly where rushed investments fail. For your capital, it's worth shielding.

What if my application is inadmitted?

We re-file it with no legal fees, as many times as needed until approval. Each new $56 USD official study is paid to the State. Final rejections not covered.
