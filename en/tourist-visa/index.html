Tourist Visa requirements

Three pillars: purchased itinerary, international insurance and hyper-verifiable solvency — we build it with you, document by document.

  • Passport valid (minimum 6 months) from a non-exempt nationality.
  • 3x4 digital color photo, white background, current and different from your passport one.
  • Bank statements from the prior 6 months with 10 minimum wages each month.
  • Purchased itinerary: inbound and outbound tickets plus hotel or tour reservations.
  • Motivation letter with full identification, schedule, places and activities.
  • Criminal records from your country of residence of the last 3 years, apostilled and translated.
  • International health insurance all-risk with repatriation, for the whole stay.

Straight answers, before you ask

Who needs this visa?

Citizens of nationalities without an exemption agreement with Colombia (India, China, Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt and dozens more). The US, Canada and the EU enter on a stamp and do not need it.

How much must I show?

Monthly income no less than 10 minimum wages ($17,468,820 COP, about $4,300-4,500 USD) in each of the prior 6 months. A single month below is enough to inadmit.

Can I work or bring beneficiaries?

Neither: this visa prohibits work and admits no beneficiaries.

How long does it last?

Up to 1 year validity, 90-day stays extendable once, with an absolute 180-day yearly cap.

What if they require more or inadmit?

Requirements allow only 10 days or the case closes. Inadmission loses the study fee ($56) and forces a restart; definitive rejection bars any visa for six months. Our inadmission reapplications carry no legal fees.

Is insurance mandatory?

Yes: full all-risk coverage inside the country for the whole stay, with repatriation.

What is the total cost?

Flat $350 USD legal fees plus official fees ($56 study + $67 type-V issuance). No invented costs: what you see is what you pay.
