Straight answers, before you ask

Does my country qualify?

Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay, Paraguay, Bolivia, Ecuador and Peru. Andean Community or another nationality? Ask on the consultation — we cover every category.

Can I work and bring family?

Yes to both: it authorizes any legal activity and covers dependents (children under 25 and dependents).

And after the 2 years?

The Mercosur visa isn't renewed — it bridges into the time-based Resident visa (those 2 years already count). We plan it from day one so you never spend a day irregular.

How long and from where do I apply?

Usually 2 to 5 weeks once filed. From Colombia or your country of residence or nationality.

What do I actually need?

Current passport, activities-and-support letter, and records from where you lived 3 years (apostilled and translated). The system's shortest file.

Do I need a lawyer if it's so simple?

No platform requires counsel. But "simple" isn't "automatic": multi-country records and the Resident bridge are where the 2 years get lost. For your regional plan, it's worth shielding.

What if my application is inadmitted?

We re-file it with no legal fees, as many times as needed until approval. Each new $56 USD official study is paid to the State. Final rejections not covered.
