Straight answers, before you ask

Does this visa let me enter Colombia?

No. It only authorizes airport transit for up to 24 hours to catch your connecting flight. You do not clear immigration and you do not enter the country — and the airline will ask for it before boarding.

Where do I apply?

Only at the Colombian consulate. It cannot be applied for from inside Colombia. In the consultation we define which consulate suits you.

Who needs it?

Citizens of nationalities that require a visitor visa for Colombia, listed in Resolution 5488 of 2022. If your nationality is not on the list, you do not need this visa: we verify it in the consultation.

What if I have no destination-country visa?

Without the visa or documents authorizing entry to your final destination, the application is denied. That is why your complete tickets plus that visa are the queen evidence: we verify them before filing.

How long does it take?

Timelines are set by the consulate where you file. Since your flight does not wait, we file with the file shielded from day one: letter with timing, tickets and complete annexes.

What if my application is deemed inadmissible?

We re-file it with no legal fees, as many times as needed until approval. Each new official study ($56 USD) is paid to the State. It does not cover definitive rejection.

What is the total cost?

Flat $540 USD legal fees plus official fees ($56 study + $67 type-V issuance). No mandatory insurance, no invented extras: what you see is what you pay.
