Proceedings Visa requirements

The file Cancillería expects to see — we build and verify it with you, document by document.

  • Request letter detailing the proceeding and why it requires your presence in Colombia.
  • Proceeding support: court orders, official directives or letters from the authorities and institutions involved.
  • Financial means: statements or other evidence you can support yourself during your stay.
  • International health insurance for the whole stay: emergencies, hospitalization and repatriation.

Straight answers, before you ask

Which proceedings qualify?

Court (lawsuits, hearings), administrative (procedures before entities) and commercial (business requiring your presence): anything needing documentary support from the authority or institution involved.

What is the cédula good for?

Operating in Colombia while your proceeding runs: bank accounts, signatures, contracts and procedures requiring local ID.

Can I work on this visa?

No: it bars working for or billing Colombian companies (passive investment income is allowed).

How long and from where do I apply?

Two to four weeks once filed — among the fastest. From Colombia or your country of residence or nationality.

What if my proceeding drags on?

The visa runs 6 months to 1 year at officer discretion; your stay strategy (extensions or category change) is planned on the consultation.

Do I need a lawyer if it's all online?

No platform requires counsel. But proving the need for your presence before a discretionary officer demands an impeccable letter and support. For your legal case, it's worth shielding them.
