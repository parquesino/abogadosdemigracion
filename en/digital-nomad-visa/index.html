Straight answers, before you ask

Do I qualify as an employee or an owner?

Both qualify. Employee: letter + contract proving remote work. Owner: company existence certificate (e.g., Certificate of Good Standing in the US) plus income proof. The consultation defines your best profile.

How much must I earn?

Over 3 Colombian monthly minimum wages, shown in 6 months of statements: $5,240,646 COP (about $1,610 USD at Oct 2026 rates).

Can I bill Colombian clients?

No. No work for Colombian companies and no local pay. Only foreign income (passive income from Colombian investments is allowed).

Does it work for staying long-term?

This visa doesn't accrue toward residency. If your goal is staying years, the consultation tells you which visa leads to the R — before you invest in the wrong one.

How long and from where do I apply?

Usually 2 to 5 weeks once filed. From Colombia or your country of residence or nationality. Granted duration (6 months to 2 years) is discretionary.

Do I need a lawyer if it's all online?

No platform requires counsel. But statements, the remote letter and insurance have details that inadmit files — as happened to several of our clients before they arrived (see reviews). For your move, it's worth shielding them.
