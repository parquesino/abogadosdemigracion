Questions agencies ask us

Who signs the engagement?

Either structure works. We can contract with your agency for the full case, or with the client directly while your team stays as the point of contact. We agree the model before the first case.

Do you contact our clients directly?

Only as agreed. Many partners prefer that all communication runs through their case manager; others ask us to handle the onshore logistics directly. Both are possible.

What if the client speaks neither English nor Spanish?

We work in English and Spanish. For clients who speak other languages, we coordinate with your case manager so instructions and appointments are understood on both sides.

What happens if the visa is rejected?

If the application is inadmissible, we re-file at no additional legal fee. A definitive rejection requires a six-month wait under Colombian rules; we explain the options and prepare the new filing when the term allows.

Do you take dependents?

Yes. In the Legal tier, dependents are $500 each. In the all-inclusive tier, dependents are quoted per case because government fees and insurance vary by age and visa type.

Are government fees really included in the all-inclusive price?

Yes: the $56 study fee, the visa issuance fee ($183 for V visas, $279 for M, $408 for R) and the cédula de extranjería ($294,000 COP) are inside the $3,200, for any visa type. We pay them and hand you the receipts.
