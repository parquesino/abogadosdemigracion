Straight answers, before you ask

Do I lose my current citizenship?

No. This visa exists precisely for that: returning to reside in Colombia while keeping the citizenship you value.

Do I need family, work or investment here?

No: unlike nearly every other visa, this one requires no ties. Only your past renunciation and means to live here.

What about 1886 Constitution cases?

If you lost nationality under art. 9 (acquired another before 1991), we prove it with your naturalization or equivalent document. Winnable cases with the right historic file.

Can I work and bring family?

Yes to both: as a permanent resident you work freely and cover dependents (art. 3 Res. 5477).

How long and from where do I apply?

Usually 2 to 5 weeks once filed. From Colombia or your country of residence or nationality.

Do I need a lawyer if it's all online?

No platform requires counsel. But proving decades-old facts before a discretionary officer demands an impeccable historic file. To come home, it's worth shielding.
