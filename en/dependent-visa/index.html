Dependent requirements

The file Cancillería expects to see — we build it together with the holder's, in one flow.

  • Copy of your current holder visa: everything starts from it.
  • Relationship record (marriage or birth certificate), apostilled and translated into Spanish if in another language.
  • Holder's letter requesting the visa and declaring economic dependence, travel/stay expenses and health coverage for the dependent.
  • Holder's financial proof: 6 months of statements or employment certification covering the whole family.

Straight answers, before you ask

Who can I bring?

Spouse or partner, children under 25 and — depending on your visa type — parents and dependent adults. All must depend on you economically and legally.

Does it last as long as mine?

Never longer than the holder's: if your visa expires or changes, your dependents' follow. That's why filing together pays off.

What if my child marries or works?

They lose dependent status and need their own holder visa. We anticipate it on the consultation so nobody falls irregular.

How long and from where do they apply?

Usually 2 to 5 weeks once filed. From Colombia or their country, 100% online and in parallel with yours.

Does everyone pay fees?

Yes: each dependent pays their study ($56), issuance and cédula ($294,000 COP), plus the reduced legal fee per your plan.

Do I need a lawyer if it's all online?

No platform requires counsel. But coordinating foreign records, economic dependence and parallel filings is where families lose months. We run it in one flow.
