The Retirement Visa in numbers

M-type visa for pension recipients who want to reside in Colombia. Apply from Colombia or your country.

Income requirementPension ≥ 3 minimum wages ($5,240,646 COP)
Duration1 to 3 years
Work allowed?No — main restriction
DependentsSpouse & children under 25
Path to residency5 accrued years → R visa
Official fees$56 study + $279 issuance + $294,000 cédula

Retirement vs. Rentista: Retirement needs a pension from 3 minimum wages and accrues toward residency; Rentista needs passive income from 10 minimum wages and doesn't accrue. The consultation confirms which one is yours.

Retirement Visa requirements

The file Cancillería expects to see — we build and verify it with you, document by document.

  • Pension certification of ≥ 3 monthly minimum wages, issued under 3 months ago by the competent authority or your consulate, apostilled and translated if applicable.
  • Criminal records from where you lived the last 3 years, apostilled with official translation.
  • Medical certificate of physical and psychological fitness, from a Colombian or home-country authority.
  • Health coverage in Colombia: insurance covering accidents, illness, hospitalization and repatriation during your stay.

Straight answers, before you ask

How large must my pension be?

At least 3 Colombian monthly minimum wages — $5,240,646 COP in 2026 (about $1,610 USD). It must be a pension (public or private), not rents or investments: those go through Rentista.

Can I work on this visa?

No. Its one big restriction: no work activity in Colombia is authorized. If you need to work, the consultation will assess whether another category suits you better.

Does it lead to permanent residency?

Yes. It's an M visa: 5 years as a holder let you apply for the Resident visa, and 5 more as a resident, citizenship.

How long and from where do I apply?

Usually 2 to 5 weeks once filed. From Colombia or your country of residence or nationality, 100% online.

Can I include my family?

Yes, as dependents (including children under 25). Each pays their own fees plus a reduced legal fee per your plan.

Do I need a lawyer if it's all online?

No platform requires counsel. But pension certification, multi-country records and the health policy have details that inadmit complete files. For your retirement, it's worth shielding them.
